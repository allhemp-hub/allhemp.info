# Why Hemp?

Industrial hemp is one of the most versatile, high-yielding, and environmentally transformative plants on Earth. While it has been utilized for thousands of years to create textiles, paper, and food, its modern applications are paving the way for a truly sustainable green revolution.

## The Triple Bottom Line of Hemp:
* **Carbon-Negative Growth:** Hemp breathes in more CO2 per acre than a traditional forest, capturing carbon rapidly in its stalks.
* **Agricultural Excellence:** It thrives without heavy pesticides, breaks up toxic soil through phytoremediation, and requires a fraction of the water that cotton demands.
* **Industrial Utility:** From biodegradable plastics and structural hempcrete to high-strength textiles and wellness products, hemp can displace fossil-fuel-reliant materials across multiple industries.
